Babasaheb's Vision for Women

Leading the Vanguard for Social Transformation

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar famously remarked, "I measure the progress of a community by the degree of progress which women have achieved." At the historic Mahad Satyagraha and the All India Depressed Classes Women’s Conference in Nagpur (1942), thousands of women resolved to throw off the chains of superstition, illiteracy, and subservience.

Mahila Samata Sainik Dal organizes women across rural and urban centers into disciplined units trained in community defense, constitutional rights, educational tutoring, and anti-domestic violence grievance resolution.

Babasaheb to Women (1942)

"Learn to be clean, keep free from all evils. Give education to your children. Instill ambition in them. Inculcate in their minds that they are destined to be great. Remove from them all inferiority complex. Never forget that you are the architect of your own destiny."

— Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Nagpur Women's Conference
Key Pillars

Operational Thrust Areas

Comprehensive community defense, legal literacy, and educational programs.

Self-Defense & Martial Arts

Regular physical fitness, unarmed self-defense, and lathi drill workshops for young girls and women.

Anti-Atrocity Legal Cell

Dedicated legal counseling and FIR registration assistance for victims of caste or domestic abuse.

Savitribai Phule Scholarships

Financial and mentoring aid for meritorious girls from marginalized backgrounds pursuing higher education.

SHG & Financial Literacy

Empowering rural women through Self-Help Group formation, banking literacy, and micro-entrepreneurship.
