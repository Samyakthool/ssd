Constitutional Shield

Upholding Dr. Ambedkar’s Constitutional Mandate

Bodhisattva Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Chief Architect of the Constitution of India, gifted the nation a supreme legal instrument founded on Equality, Liberty, and Fraternity. The SSD Legal Advisory Cell was established to bridge the gap between constitutional provisions and ground reality.

Our network comprises over 850+ practicing advocates across the Supreme Court of India, State High Courts, and District Sessions Courts who provide voluntary, pro-bono representation for victims of caste atrocities, illegal land dispossession, and discrimination.

Key Constitutional Mandates We Defend

  • Article 14 & 15: Equality before law and prohibition of discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex or place of birth.
  • Article 17: Total abolition of Untouchability and strict punishment under criminal law.
  • Article 21: Protection of life, personal liberty, and right to live with human dignity.
  • Article 32 & 226: Constitutional remedies via Writ Petitions (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Certiorari).
  • SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989: Immediate FIR registration, special court trials, and victim compensation.

Public Interest Litigations

Filing PILs in High Courts and Supreme Court on reservation policy enforcement, student scholarships, and land rights.

Constitutional Literacy Camps

Conducting village-level workshops on citizen rights, police encounter protocols, and RTI Act usage.
