Direct Enlistment

Join Samata Sainik Dal — Take the Solemn Pledge

Answer the historic call of Bodhisattva Babasaheb Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. Become a disciplined volunteer for equality, human dignity, and constitutional morality.

4-Step Enlistment Process

1

Select Your Operational Wing

Choose from Cadet Corps (Drill & Discipline), Mahila Dal (Women's Leadership), Legal Cell (Advocacy), Youth Front, or Sewa Relief.

2

Submit District & Contact Details

Provide your local address to be connected directly with your District Commander and local State Chapter.

3

Take the Solemn Volunteer Pledge

Commit to the principles of self-respect, moral character, non-violence, and total dedication to liberty, equality, and fraternity.

4

Receive SSD Digital ID & Unit Assignment

Central Command verifies your application, registers you in the national roll, and invites you to the next drill camp.
