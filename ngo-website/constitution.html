SOLEMN DECLARATION

PREAMBLE (प्रस्तावना)

"We, the members and volunteer sainiks of Samata Sainik Dal, solemnly resolve to dedicate our lives to the realization of an egalitarian society free from the scourge of caste, inequality, and untouchability; to defend human rights, social dignity, and constitutional morality through non-violent discipline; and to organize the oppressed millions under the banner of Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity as proclaimed by Bodhisattva Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar."
