The Supreme Purpose

Official Aim (ध्येय)

To organize, educate, and discipline the oppressed and marginalized masses; to safeguard constitutional morality, civil liberties, and human dignity; to eliminate the caste system and social untouchability; and to establish an enlightened democratic society based on Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity as proclaimed by Bodhisattva Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.

Approved by Central Command & Supreme Directorate, Nagpur.
The Sainik Creed

Official Motto (बोधवाक्य)

"Self-Respect, Discipline, Equality & Social Service"

Guiding Revolutionary Command:

"Educate, Agitate, Organize — Guard the Constitution"
Historical Creed promulgated at Mahad Conference, 1927.

Vision (दृष्टी)

An egalitarian, caste-free India where every individual enjoys equal civil rights, social justice, and constitutional protections without fear of tyranny or oppression.

Mission (ध्येयकार्य)

Building a nationwide volunteer cadre of enlightened, disciplined sainiks dedicated to non-violent defense of human rights, legal literacy, relief during disasters, and educational emancipation.

Statutory Charter

Discipline, Non-Violence & Service

Samata Sainik Dal is not a political party; it is a permanent non-violent volunteer corps. Every Sainik is required to maintain sober, upright character, abstain from communal hatred, respect the chain of command, and report for duty in official uniform during assemblies, national drills, and civic emergencies.
