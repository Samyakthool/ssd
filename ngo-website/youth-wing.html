Youth & Student Empowerment Front (युवा मोर्चा)

Fostering intellectual excellence, scientific temper, competitive examination guidance, and fearless leadership among the youth of India.

Home / Departments / Youth & Student Front
Educate • Agitate • Organize

Empowering the Next Generation of Intellectual Cadres

Babasaheb Dr. B.R. Ambedkar believed that real liberation comes through rigorous modern education and intellectual mastery. The Youth & Student Front of Samata Sainik Dal operates on campuses and in rural communities to channel the vibrant energy of students into constructive nation-building and social justice.

We run Dr. Ambedkar study circles, organize free coaching and study groups for UPSC, State PSCs, SSC, and Banking exams, and teach critical digital literacy to first-generation learners.

Key Programs

Core Initiatives for Students & Youth

Guiding youth from secondary schools to higher research and administrative careers.

Ambedkar Study Circles

Weekly reading groups analyzing the Writings & Speeches of Dr. Ambedkar, Constitution, and socio-economic history.

Civil Services Mentorship

Free mentoring, mock interviews, and test series for civil service aspirants led by serving civil servants and senior Sainiks.

Debate & Leadership Conclaves

Annual national elocution, public speaking, and youth policy conferences to nurture articulate public leaders.

Digital Skills & AI Labs

Workshops on programming, web development, and digital entrepreneurship for first-generation college students.

Start a Samata Sainik Dal Youth Chapter on Your Campus

Establish an active study circle or cadet team in your university, college, or district to promote constitutional values and student solidarity.
