Selfless Service

Disaster Response & Humanitarian Relief

The Community Sewa and Relief Task Force operates on the foundational principle that a true Sainik of Dr. Ambedkar is always ready to serve humanity in distress. From floods in Maharashtra and Bihar to earthquake relief and pandemic medical outreach, SSD volunteers act as disciplined first responders.

We maintain an active registry of voluntary blood donors, organize free health screening camps in remote villages, distribute relief kits to vulnerable habitations, and establish community libraries.

Relief Task Force Capabilities

  • Voluntary Blood Network: Over 15,000+ verified voluntary blood donors across 28 states.
  • Mobile Medical Vans: Free health checkups, blood pressure & sugar screening, and generic medicines.
  • Emergency Relief Packs: Ration kits, clean drinking water, thermal blankets, and sanitary pads during floods.
  • Dr. Ambedkar Reading Rooms: Over 250+ free village study rooms furnished with competitive exam books.
Relief Initiatives

Core Humanitarian Operations

Dedicated grassroots programs delivering tangible relief and medical aid.

24/7 Blood Donation Task Force

State-level emergency response teams ready to donate blood and platelets for critical patients in government hospitals.

Disaster Rescue Brigades

Trained cadets equipped for flood evacuation, debris clearance, and temporary shelter setup during natural disasters.

Free Diagnostic Camps

Volunteer doctors and nurses conducting eye camps, pediatric checkups, and nutritional counseling for rural children.

Gramin Ambedkar Libraries

Setting up community reading rooms in Dalit and tribal habitations with free Wi-Fi and school tutoring.
