The Cadet Tradition

Discipline, Physical Vigilance, and Social Courage

The Central Cadet Corps is the primary uniformed wing of Samata Sainik Dal. Ever since the historic 1927 Mahad Satyagraha and the 1956 Deekshabhoomi Dhamma Deeksha, SSD Cadets have provided selfless, non-violent protection to the community, mass assemblies, and Dr. Ambedkar's democratic movement.

Cadets undergo intensive training in marching drills, synchronized flag salutes, self-defense tactics, crowd control, and first-aid emergency rescue.

Official Uniform Specifications

  • Shirt & Trouser: White or Khaki ceremonial uniform with epaulettes.
  • Headgear: Royal Blue Beret / Cap with the Brass Dhamma Wheel Insignia.
  • Belt & Footwear: Black leather belt with SSD buckle and polished marching boots.
  • Chest Badge: "समता सैनिक दल - SAMATA SAINIK DAL" engraved on the right pocket.
  • Ceremonial Lanyard: Deep blue lanyard with command whistle.
Training Curriculum

Core Cadet Training Modules

Rigorous physical, moral, and legal training administered across regional SSD training camps.

March Past & Drill

Foot drills, squad marching, flag hoisting ceremonial protocols, and parade synchronization.

Non-Violent Self Defense

Lathi defense techniques, unarmed physical combat defense, and situational de-escalation skills.

Constitutional Morality

Study of the Preamble, Fundamental Rights (Articles 14 to 32), and Dr. Ambedkar's democratic philosophy.

First Aid & Rescue

Emergency medical triage, CPR, disaster evacuation, and crowd management in large rallies.

Command Structure

Cadet Corps Rank Hierarchy

A disciplined command structure ensuring smooth coordination across all 28 State Chapters.

1. Sainik (सैनिक)

Enlisted cadet volunteer who has completed basic drill training and sworn the solemn equality pledge.

2. Nayak / Squad Leader

Leads a unit of 15 to 25 cadets at the local ward, mohalla, or village level.

3. District Commander

Oversees cadet deployment, drills, and rally security across the entire revenue district.

4. State / National General

Member of the Central Command Secretariat coordinating statewide and national assemblies.
